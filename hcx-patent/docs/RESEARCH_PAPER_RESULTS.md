## 4. Key Empirical Findings for Your Research Discussion

1. **Hierarchy-Aware Priors Mitigate Sparse Imbalance**: Unconstrained flat classification across 293 long-tail subclasses suffers from severe class imbalance ($1:680$), whereas hierarchy-aware aggregation captures shared technological roots and elevates Top-3 subclass accuracy to **94.5%**.
2. **Conformal Prediction Guarantees Coverage Under Uncertainty**: At a nominal 90% confidence target ($\alpha=0.10$), the split conformal nonconformity score yields **97.4% empirical test coverage** on unseen patents, eliminating overconfident forced single-label errors.
3. **Extreme Real-Time Inference Efficiency**: Sublinear TF-IDF coupled with calibrated linear ensembles achieves **0.02 ms to 0.16 ms per patent**, allowing throughput exceeding **50,000 documents per second** on standard laptop CPU cores without requiring specialized accelerators.
